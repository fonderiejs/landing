Every session, your AI starts from zero.

Regenerates from scratch

03 — Proof

2 of 3

AI-generated SaaS backends we tested shipped a real security hole. Fonderie's didn't.

We built the same SaaS three times from scratch with AI assistants — once composing Fonderie modules, twice without. The Fonderie build had zero vulnerabilities, in about a third of the code.
